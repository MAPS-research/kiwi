Kiwi UI refresh · Stage 1 · Phase 2; Design DNA and light/dark tokens · 2026-10-16 · comparison sheet for the pick (UPDATE_PLAN round-trip)

Five dealt hands on the pinned hue

Pins (user law): hue 156 (mascot forest, measured) · chroma muted · one humanist sans · Montserrat only in the wordmark · light and dark · cream-family canvas · quiet shell, one living companion

Hue and chroma are pinned, so the colour tokens are the same on all five candidates; accent, neutral and functional ramps come from three recorded palette.mjs runs (seed 156 muted → accent; seed 95 muted → cream/tan-tinted neutrals and the paper canvas steps; seed 156 balanced → functional colours, so success is more chromatic than primary, G1). What you are choosing between is the family and composition stance, the type face, the motion vocabulary and the signature move, plus small differences in how deep the cream canvas goes and how sheets sit on it. Colour roles follow contrast, not habit: in light mode the filled control is forest (--accent-12; palette.mjs's own solid --accent-9 is sage at L 0.78 and measures 1.7:1 against cream, below the 3:1 boundary rule), in dark mode it is sage (--accent-9). Links are --accent-11 in both modes.

Dealer: dealer.mjs --project kiwi-ui --date 2026-10-16 --used .design-foundations/used-dna.json --pin hue=156 --pin chroma=muted (deal 0) and the same with --reroll 1 (deal 1). Both deals are in the ledger. Hands whose family cannot serve the pinned feel (brand-only register; colour and type axes nullified by the pins; layout/motion against the Feel) were re-dealt on the record, never edited: deal 0; Retro-Futurist × Monolith Center, Playful Geometric × Fractured Grid, Cinematic Dark × Poster Bleed; deal 1; Cinematic Dark × Marginalia, Art Deco / Luxury × Frieze Bands. Soft Margin

Soft Futurism × Marginalia (variance 5) · signature box-drawing-borders · face Nunito Sans · seed kiwi-ui|2026-10-16|0|pin:chroma=muted,hue=156
Runner-up
GROUNDING
Edward Tufte's sidenotes in Beautiful Evidence: the margin as working space beside the text, not footnotes below it + Things 3 (Cultured Code): calm layered white sheets, generous radius, one accent
Citations and feedback get a home beside the answer instead of under it, on sheets that float the way the chat panel already does.
TYPE
Nunito Sans over Nunito Sans: soft terminals without rounding into a toy; weights 400/700; the "neutral-but-warm" sans the family asks for, minus Instrument Sans (overused list)
COLOR
seed green 156 (pinned) · pale cream atmosphere (--paper-2) with floating white sheets (--neutral-1); dark: canvas --paper-2, sheets --neutral-3 · mono harmony · forest-tinted soft shadow via color-mix on --accent-on-solid · swatches #203327 #84ca9e #faf9f3
COMPOSITION
<dealt>: Soft Futurism × Marginalia, dial 5 · justification: the chat answer gains an active margin for "[Page N]" and feedback; the admin two-column forms (Class settings) already are column + margin, so the stance fits what the tour praised
MOTION
fluid: 120 ms micro, 320 ms standard, 420 ms for layer order (panel scale 0.97→1 + fade), ease-out-expo; toggles ease-in-out; the margin fades in after the answer
REGISTER
calm structure · expressive at: the chat panel's arrival (layer motion), sign-in (one floating sheet + mascot), empty and not-found states (mascot on a sheet)
DNA
Soft Futurism base + color strategy from Editorial Minimalism (warm paper and one muted accent instead of a tinted atmosphere) · dominant: composition (the working margin)
SIGNATURE
Tables and rules use box-drawing characters as borders: code blocks and tables are framed by ┌ ┐ └ ┘ corner glyphs; the margin's rule is drawn in │

Strongest: Marginalia gives citations and feedback a real home beside the answer and matches the two-column admin forms the tour already praised; the only hand that uses the chat's unused width.

Tells: glassmorphism is the family's named ⚠; avoided by value (no backdrop-filter) and guarded in Never. Nearest cluster: calm-productivity (Things 3 itself); the margin apparatus is the difference.

Where the stance lives in Stage 1: chat answers (citations and feedback in the margin), Class settings and the other two-column admin forms, the PDF annotation sidebar. Tables keep square corners inside the sheet.

GROUNDING
Kew Herbarium specimen sheets: one pressed specimen centred on cream paper, a small typed label in the corner + Muji packaging (Kenya Hara): unbleached-paper neutrals, one word of type, no decoration
The mascot becomes the single centred specimen on the moments that can carry it, and everything else is a quiet label on unbleached paper.
TYPE
Alegreya Sans over Alegreya Sans: a calligraphic humanist drawn for literature; weights 400/700; it carries the organic feel alone now that the family's serif body is pinned out
COLOR
seed green 156 (pinned) · unbleached canvas (--paper-3) with paper sheets (--paper-2), tonal: no borders, no shadows; dark: canvas --paper-2, sheets --paper-3 · mono harmony · swatches #203327 #84ca9e #f4f1e4
COMPOSITION
<dealt>: Organic / Natural × Monolith Center, dial 1 · justification: the empty states, sign-in and not-found pages get one centred mass (the mascot) with everything else caption-rank; inside dense pages the stance becomes "one focus per region" (the next step, nothing competing)
MOTION
slow and material: 150 ms micro, 320 ms standard, one 450 ms settle (scale 0.96→1, ease-in-out) for the centred mass on empty states and sign-in; tool chrome never above 150 ms
REGISTER
calm structure · expressive at: empty states (the specimen), sign-in (the specimen), not-found (the specimen); the chat companion stays a 32 px label-rank avatar
DNA
Organic / Natural base + type voice from Warm Editorial (humanist sans in place of the humanist serif) · dominant: colour (moss and cream naturals: the family's home ground)

Where the stance lives in Stage 1: the no-classes and no-conversation empty states, the class-card fallback, sign-in and not-found (one centred mascot, label-rank copy); elsewhere one focus per region and tonal sheets without borders.

A Moleskine notebook: cream paper, rounded corners, tactile restraint + Saul Bass's 1955 poster for The Man with the Golden Arm: one cut-paper figure fills the sheet, the title sits small in a corner
Warm paper and soft corners for the student's hands, and one place; the auth panel and the not-found page; where the mascot fills the sheet and the words step aside.

Fira Sans over Fira Sans: the FF Meta lineage: warm, legible at small sizes, 16 weights with true italics; weights 400/500/600
COLOR
seed green 156 (pinned) · warm paper canvas (--paper-3) with paper sheets (--paper-2) and a deeper paper band (--paper-4) as the bleed ground; dark: canvas --paper-2, sheets --paper-3, band --paper-4 · mono harmony · tan-warm shadow via color-mix · swatches #203327 #84ca9e #ede8d5
COMPOSITION
<dealt>: Warm Editorial × Poster Bleed, dial 8 · justification: the auth split already has a full-height image panel and the not-found route is free to restyle: the mascot bleeds there at poster scale; everywhere else the stance collapses to a ground band with copy pinned to its edge

Set Square

Swiss / International × Swiss Modular (variance 3) · signature hard-offset-shadow · face IBM Plex Sans · seed kiwi-ui|2026-10-16|1|pin:chroma=muted,hue=156
GROUNDING
Massimo Vignelli's 1970s New York City subway signage (Unimark): a flush-left grid where colour is only the line's identity + Hans Hilfiker's Swiss railway clock: one red element on a monochrome dial, the second hand's pause as the only motion
Colour carries meaning only and motion happens once, on purpose; the brief's own rules, set on an 8 px module grid that dense admin pages can live on.
TYPE
IBM Plex Sans over IBM Plex Sans: a grotesque with humanist traits (open apertures, modulated stroke), the only way Swiss discipline and the humanist pin meet; weights 400/500/600; exemplary tabular figures
COLOR
seed green 156 (pinned) · light cream canvas (--paper-2) with white sheets (--neutral-1) separated by hairlines, no containers; dark: canvas --paper-2, sheets --neutral-3 · mono harmony · no shadows except the signature's hard offset · swatches #203327 #84ca9e #faf9f3
COMPOSITION
<dealt>: Swiss / International × Swiss Modular, dial 3 · justification: every page keeps its regions and gains one module grid (8 px), hairline dividers and flush-left rag-right; admin tables are its home and student pages hold the same grid at comfortable density
MOTION
instant: 100 ms micro, 150 ms standard, no orchestration; linear or sharp ease-out; only state changes move (press, toggle, selection); no entrances anywhere
REGISTER
calm structure · expressive at: the chat companion (mascot), sign-in (one display line on the grid + mascot), empty and not-found states (mascot on the grid): modest amplitude throughout
DNA
Swiss / International base + type voice from Data-Dense Professional (compact sans with mandatory tabular numerals) · dominant: composition (the module grid)
SIGNATURE
All interactive elements share one hard offset shadow that shifts on press: a 2 px × 2 px offset in the boundary colour on buttons, inputs and chips; on press the element moves into it and the shadow disappears

Strongest: Vignelli's "colour is information" is the brief's own rule and Swiss Modular suits ~25 dense admin pages; the 100–150 ms budget is the most honest tool-chrome motion of the five; Plex's tabular figures are exemplary.

Weakest: the humanist pin removes the grotesque that makes Swiss Swiss; Plex is a hybrid, so the type voice sits between two structures; zero radius and no shadows run cold for the Caregiver side; the dealt hard-offset shadow is Neo-Brutalist in origin and sits against "borders before shadows"; the likeliest converge-time swap.

Tells: none hit by value. Nearest cluster: the flat hairline dashboard (Vercel / Linear); the forest fill, cream canvas and Plex keep distance, but this is the candidate closest to a generic centre.

Where the stance lives in Stage 1: everywhere; the 8 px module grid, hairline dividers, flush-left type; admin tables are its home; the hard offset on every control.
