Mode: light · plot background

1 · The five series and the neutral (DW-6.1)

Slots in their fixed order. Each swatch prints, on the sheet under its colour, its hex, its OKLCH lightness, chroma and hue, and its lowest contrast on the three surfaces a mark can sit on (the plot, the canvas, the hover row), naming which; the table prints the pairwise OKLab distance (× 100) under normal vision and under the Machado 2009 protanopia and deuteranopia simulations, recomputed from the rendered tokens. Gates: ≥ 3:1; ≥ 8 under simulation; ≥ 15 under normal vision.

3 · The KPI tile applied (DW-6.3)

Label, value and a third line: a week-on-week trend where the page already has the weekly series (the direction glyph in the line's own colour, hidden from assistive technology, and the direction in the words), the denominator where the value is a ratio, and the statement that there is none otherwise. The icon is always --icon-default.

4 · An admin table sample (DW-6.5)

Token usage detail, in its dialog at two widths: right-aligned tabular numerals with the unit in the header and nowhere else (one place per column), fixed-format dates, and an identifier in monospace at the scale's code size. Each column takes its content's width where the dialog allows (the dates at --size-col-min-date, the numbers at the wider of their header and --size-col-min-num) and the id column takes the rest, so the id is cut only where the dialog is narrower than it needs: there its span takes a tab stop and the tooltip (a sibling of the clipped text, never clipped by it, with the toggletip geometry, the cell's monospace, below the inset focus ring, reachable by the pointer across the offset and closed by Escape). The em dash named "No value" marks an open session. Compact rows (36 px).

The dialog at full width (maxWidth="md", 900 px): the session id fits whole, so it has no tab stop and no tooltip.

5 · Near-empty demo data (the edge case)

A chart whose series is empty shows the empty-state line in the plot's place, never a zero-filled curve or six zero bars; a series with one or two non-zero buckets (days, or the hours of a 24-bucket hourly series) draws them as bars, each labelled with its value, with no line.
