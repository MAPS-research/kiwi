Mode: light · plot background

1 · The five series and the neutral (DW-6.1)

Slots in their fixed order. Each swatch prints its hex, its OKLCH lightness, chroma and hue, and its lowest contrast on the three surfaces a mark can sit on (the plot, the canvas, the hover row), naming which; the table prints the pairwise OKLab distance (× 100) under normal vision and under the Machado 2009 protanopia and deuteranopia simulations, recomputed from the rendered tokens. Gates: ≥ 3:1; ≥ 8 under simulation; ≥ 15 under normal vision.

3 · The KPI tile applied (DW-6.3)

Label, value and a third line: a named baseline where the page has the data, the denominator where the value is a ratio, and the statement that there is none otherwise. The icon is always --icon-default.

4 · An admin table sample (DW-6.5)

Token usage detail: right-aligned tabular numerals with the unit in the header and nowhere else (one place per column), fixed-format dates, an identifier truncated to one line with the full text on hover and focus (the span is focusable; the tooltip is a sibling of the clipped text, never clipped by it), and the em dash named "No value" for an open session.
