Companion sheet — the tutor mascot (Phase 4, amended 2026-10-18)

The user's decisions of 2026-10-18: the tutor avatar is always the small, full-body bird. It runs from the send until the answer is complete, flies once, and otherwise stands, in one framing for every state. Everything else of kiwi-ui/DESIGN.md § Companion stands. Drawings: NYUSH-ML/kiwi-ui@fbb4db8 (assets/mascot/provenance.md): whole drawings stepped on a 24 Hz clock, nothing tweened.

Your device asks for reduced motion Motion: on

A lower level never overrides a higher one: with the flag off the switch is hidden; with reduced motion the switch is inert (aria-disabled, its reason as its description). With motion off at any level every avatar shows the standing still, the text twins carry every state, and the composer peek is removed.

1 · One answer, end to end

The newest tutor avatar at its real size (32 px), from rest to rest. One framing for every full-body drawing (on the frame: left 4.493 %, top 7.887 %, size 78.981 % of the slot), so the figure keeps its size and place and only the drawing changes. Idle → send → the run while Kiwi waits → the same run while the answer streams (no restart at the first token) → one hop and flight at completion, from the current run drawing at the next tick → landed on the standing still, which is the idle still. The line under each panel names the state and what the student sees or hears.

The error and offline stills

No full-body error or sleep drawing exists, and none is invented. Both states rest on the standing still and their text twins carry them: the error row with role="alert", the composer's offline line with role="status". Considered and not used:

light
dark

The bow is the library's heart (thanks): it reads as thanks as easily as an apology, and a run that cuts into a bow is a large jump. The crouch is the hop's anticipation: it reads as “about to jump”, which claims an answer is coming when none can. The standing still claims nothing.

5 · Motion off: the still of every state

What every motion-off level shows (the flag, reduced motion, or the switch): the standing still in every avatar state, so the avatar does not change at all and the text twins carry every state. The composer peek is removed, not stilled; hop and wave are skipped.

LevelControlOff means
1VITE_ENABLE_MASCOT_MOTION (build)the standing still on every row; no peek; the Display menu hides the switch; no atlas is fetched
2prefers-reduced-motion: reduce (OS)the standing still; no peek; the switch is drawn inert (aria-disabled) with “Your device asks for reduced motion” as its description
3Display menu · Kiwi animation (device)the standing still; no peek; default on
