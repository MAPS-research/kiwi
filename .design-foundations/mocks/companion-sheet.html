Companion sheet — the status line, the original logo, the round launcher (companion v2)

The user's decisions of 2026-10-18 and 2026-10-19: the Kiwi's run, flight and stillness live in the newest tutor message's status line, as in the Claude Code desktop app, and the tutor's messages carry no icon. Every other placement draws the original logo as it is; the launcher is the logo in round form. The composer peek is unchanged and the like wave is dropped. Drawings: NYUSH-ML/kiwi-ui@fbb4db8 (assets/mascot/provenance.md), whole drawings stepped on a 24 Hz clock, nothing tweened. Spec: kiwi-ui/DESIGN.md § Companion and § Voice and tone › The status line.

Your device asks for reduced motion Motion: on

A lower level never overrides a higher one: with the flag off the switch is hidden; with reduced motion the switch is inert (aria-disabled, its reason as its description). With motion off at any level every line shows the standing still beside its words, goes at completion with no flight, and the composer peek is removed.

1 · One answer, end to end: the status line

The first answer of a chat, at its real size. No icon beside the tutor's messages: the text begins at the reading gutter, 24 px right of the ruler, and the newest message's toolbar row (28 px) holds the line while the turn runs: the Kiwi runs in place in a 24 px box, its words 8 px to its right in --companion-line-ink (--accent-text). One 24 Hz clock from the send: the wait verb, then the step's own words, then “Jotting it down…” over the streaming text, with no restart of the run. At completion the words go, the toolbar's buttons appear, the Kiwi flies once at the next tick (24 drawings and a 4-tick hold, 1.17 s), then the line fades out in 176 ms (opacity only) and the citation takes its place. Send became Stop inside the composer box at the send.

3 · The line's words (Playful)

Static words, no dots, never a live region (a screen reader meets them when it reads the message, and is never interrupted by a rotating verb). Reading steps keep their object with a Kiwi verb; acting and tool steps stay plain, because “for you” and a tool's name are information. Nothing counts, estimates, promises speed or claims effort.
