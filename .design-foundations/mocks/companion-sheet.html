Every state plays from the resampled atlases with the timelines of kiwi-ui/DESIGN.md § Companion; each placement at its slot size; light and dark side by side with the backing; the motion controls in their precedence. Drawings: NYUSH-ML/kiwi-ui@fbb4db8, assets/mascot/provenance.md. md is the MUI md breakpoint, a 900 px viewport.

Your device asks for reduced motion Motion: on

A lower level never overrides a higher one: with the flag off the switch is hidden; with reduced motion the switch is inert (aria-disabled, its reason as its description) and every state shows its still; the composer peek is removed, not stilled.

128 px inspection stage (the 128 px atlas set) on the light canvas and on the dark canvas with the backing disc; beside each, the still (64 px) that every motion-off level shows. 3 · Motion off: the still of every state

What the avatar shows at any motion-off level (the flag, reduced motion, or the switch). State changes still read as cuts between these stills; hop and wave, which end in idle, are skipped altogether (the avatar goes straight to the idle still), while error and offline show their still at once and hold it as long as the state lasts. The peek has a still for the inventory but is not placed when motion is off.

LevelControlOff means
1VITE_ENABLE_MASCOT_MOTION (build)idle still on every row; no peek; the Display menu hides the switch; no atlas is fetched
2prefers-reduced-motion: reduce (OS)stills; no peek; the switch is drawn inert (aria-disabled) with "Your device asks for reduced motion" as its description
3Display menu · Kiwi animation (device)stills; no peek; default on
